# Superseded

This paper ("Game Deserved Score: Decomposing NFL Team Quality into Offensive, Defensive, and
Special Teams Value") has been merged with `papers/paper3-archetypes/` into a single manuscript:

**`papers/paper2-merged/`** — "Game Deserved Score: Decomposing NFL Team Quality and Testing
the Defensive Dominance Hypothesis"

The merged paper reports the same GDS framework and validation on an extended, 256-team-season
(2018-2025) sample, and includes the archetype/hypothesis-testing analysis that was previously
`papers/paper3-archetypes/`. This folder is kept for historical reference.
